Challenge Checklist

  • 3-Rung Cap: You cannot challenge someone >3 spots above you.
  • 48h Deadline: Defending player must respond within 48 hours.
  • Color Choice: Defender picks piece color for game 1.
  • Swap Rule: Challenger takes defender's rung on victory.
