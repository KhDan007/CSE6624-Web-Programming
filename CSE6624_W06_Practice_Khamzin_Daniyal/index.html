Satbayev Varsity Volleyball: Net Dominance

About Drills Schedule

Card 1 — Three-Step Swing Block

Explosive lateral movement along the net. Swing arms forcefully upwards to maximize vertical elevation and seal the seam.

Card 2 — Quick-1 Middle Attack

Fast tempo set right at the setter's hands. The middle blocker jumps before the set is released to bypass the opposing block.

Card 3 — Slide Approach & Finish

Running behind the setter with a one-footed takeoff. Creates mismatches against taller opposing blockers along Zone 2.

Card 4 — Joust & Net Recovery

Contesting tight overpasses with two hands penetrating across the tape while maintaining vertical body posture.
