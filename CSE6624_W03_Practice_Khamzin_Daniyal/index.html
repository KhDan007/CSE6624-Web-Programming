How I Train as a Varsity Middle Blocker on Saturdays

Balancing computer science coursework and collegiate athletics requires structured Saturday sessions focused on lateral quickness, jump recovery, and net discipline.

Tools & Equipment I Keep Ready

  • Mizuno Wave Momentum volleyball shoes for maximum court grip
  • Active ankle braces for landing stability during contested net battles
  • Resistance bands for rotator cuff warm-ups before hitting
  • Official Mikasa V200W match volleyballs

Three Steps in My Daily Routine

  1. Dynamic warm-up and lateral plyometric ladder drills
  2. Reading setter release angles and executing swing-block closures
  3. Quick transition offense hitting high-tempo 1s and shoot sets
