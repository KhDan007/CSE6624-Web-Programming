Collegiate Middle Blocker Program

Satbayev University's varsity volleyball team competes in the National Student League. As middle blockers, we dominate the net through disciplined swing-block seals and high-velocity quick attacks that freeze opponent defenses.

Training Arena & Facility

Practices occur at Sports Complex Court A. State-of-the-art taraflex surfaces, regulation nets, and plyometric equipment support our high-impact training cycles.
