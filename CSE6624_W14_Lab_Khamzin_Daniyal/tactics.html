Technical footwork breakdowns and weekly training rotations for the Satbayev varsity volleyball squad.

Middle Blocker Tactical Keys

1. Three-Step Swing Footwork: Directional plant step followed by a deep crossover and explosive plant to seal the pin.

2. Eye Tracking Sequence: Pass-to-setter trajectory, setter hips/hands, ball release line, and final spiker approach.
