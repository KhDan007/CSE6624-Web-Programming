Collegiate Middle Blocker Program

Satbayev University's varsity volleyball team competes in the National Student League. As middle blockers, our primary mission is anchoring net defense, sealing seams against perimeter spikers, and running high-velocity quick attacks that freeze the opponent's defensive block.

Training Schedule

Practices run every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from 18:00 to 20:30 at the Satbayev Sports Complex Arena. Sessions focus on lateral agility, swing blocking footwork, and transition attacking.

Featured Facility

Court A provides collegiate-standard taraflex flooring, competition netting, and specialized Vertec vertical jump testing apparatus.
